# Results

Generated by `python experiment.py`. Every number below comes from that run.

## 1. Double DQN agent

Trained for 5000 episodes on randomly generated levels (6.6 min on CPU). Mean episode reward rose from -4.37 (first 100) to 4.39 (last 100). Evaluated on 500 new levels per row, all policies on the same levels.

| Level | Random win | DQN win | DQN trap | DQN gems | Planner win |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| easy (5x5, 5% traps) | 3% | **96%** | 0% | 2.67 | 100% |
| medium (6x6, 15%) | 0% | **86%** | 0% | 2.37 | 100% |
| hard (7x7, 30%) | 0% | **56%** | 0% | 1.80 | 100% |

Planner = shortest safe path with full knowledge of the map. It is the upper bound; the agent only sees a 5x5 window.

## 2. Predicting whether a player wins a level

400 simulated players with different skill and play styles produced 10000 sessions (overall win rate 61%). The split is by player: test players never appear in training.

| Model | ROC-AUC (higher is better) | Log loss (lower) | Brier (lower) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Always predict the average | 0.500 | 0.668 | 0.238 |
| Logistic regression (scikit-learn) | 0.775 | 0.553 | 0.186 |
| Neural network (PyTorch) | 0.779 | 0.548 | 0.185 |

## 3. Dynamic difficulty

200 new players each play 30 levels. Target win rate: 70%. Fixed = every level is 6x6 with 20% traps. Adaptive = before each level the director picks the board size (4 to 8) and trap ratio (0% to 40%) whose predicted win chance for this player is closest to the target, using the PyTorch win model.

| Players | Count | Fixed difficulty win rate | Adaptive win rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| lower skill (0.50-0.65) | 73 | 29% | **56%** |
| middle skill (0.65-0.80) | 62 | 53% | **65%** |
| higher skill (0.80-0.95) | 65 | 79% | **77%** |
